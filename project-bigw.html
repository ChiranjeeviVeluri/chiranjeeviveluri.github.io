About This Project

What Was Built & Why

BIG W operates across both physical stores and an E-Retail channel — but without a unified view, comparing their performance requires digging through raw data manually. This project transformed a multi-year sales dataset into a fully interactive Excel dashboard that enables instant channel comparison at the click of a slicer.

Using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPI cards, and slicers, the dashboard lets stakeholders explore trends across financial years, states, product categories, top buyers, and managers — all from a single screen.

  • Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts
  • Interactive Slicers (Year, State, Channel)
  • KPI Summary Cards
  • Line, Column, Bar & Doughnut Charts
  • Monthly Top Selling Products Analysis
  • Dashboard Layout & Visual Design
Problem Statement

The Business Challenge

No Unified Channel View

Store and E-Retail sales data existed separately, making side-by-side performance comparison time-consuming and error-prone.

Inventory Blind Spots

Without visibility into monthly top-selling products, seasonal demand spikes led to stock shortages and overstocking in key categories.

Unrecognised Top Performers

High-value buyers and managers driving disproportionate revenue had no visibility, missing opportunities for retention and recognition.

Methodology

How It Was Done

01

Data Preparation

Cleaned and structured the raw sales dataset covering transactions across multiple Australian states, financial years, suburbs, categories, managers, and buyers.

02

Pivot Table Modelling

Built pivot tables to aggregate sales by channel (Store vs E-Retail), financial year, state, product category, buyer, and manager — forming the data backbone of the dashboard.

03

Dashboard Design

Connected pivot charts (Line, Column, Bar, Doughnut) and KPI cards to the pivot models. Added slicers to allow dynamic filtering across all visuals simultaneously.

04

Monthly Product Analysis

Identified the top-selling product category per month to surface seasonal demand patterns and inform inventory and promotional planning.

Key Findings

Business Insights

01
Store Dominates Revenue

Physical store sales contributed the majority of total revenue, confirming the continued importance of in-store retail for BIG W's business model.

02
Gaming, Tech & Audio Leads

This category emerged as the highest-performing across most months, driven by consistent consumer demand in electronics and gaming.

03
High-Value Suburbs Identified

Certain suburbs consistently outperformed others, highlighting key locations for targeted marketing and inventory prioritisation.

04
Seasonal Demand Patterns

Monthly top-product analysis revealed clear seasonal peaks — actionable for promotions, stock planning, and demand forecasting.

Recommendations

Actionable Insights for BIG W

Optimise Inventory Planning

Use monthly top-selling product trends to pre-stock high-demand categories before seasonal peaks, reducing shortages and overstock costs.

Grow E-Retail Investment

While Store leads revenue, E-Retail shows opportunity for growth. Targeted digital promotions in high-performing categories can close the gap.

Focus on High-Value Locations

Prioritise stock allocation, staffing, and promotions for suburbs that consistently drive above-average sales performance.

Recognise Top Performers

Top buyers and managers contribute disproportionately to revenue. Retention strategies and performance incentives can safeguard this revenue stream.

Plan Promotions Around Peaks

Monthly sales patterns indicate optimal windows for promotions. Aligning discount campaigns with demand peaks maximises conversion and margin.

Expand Dashboard Scope

Integrating returns, profit margins, and online traffic data would deepen the analysis and enable more precise channel-level decision making.
