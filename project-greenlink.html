About This Project

What Was Built & Why

Greenlink Sandbelt Indigenous Nursery is a volunteer-run community nursery publishing a public plant stock list, but with no structured way to compare what they can propagate against seasonal demand. This project turns that single public CSV into a working planning tool — cleaning and modelling the data in Excel, then layering forecasting and what-if scenarios on top in Power BI.

Species-level stock was combined with a seasonal flowering reference table and a manually cross-referenced council planting demand list, so volunteers can see not just what's in stock, but what's seasonally relevant and locally recommended to grow.

Problem Statement

The Planning Challenge

No Capacity Visibility

Volunteers had no way to see propagation demand against real bench space, strike rate, or potting-on lead time before committing seed and cuttings.

Unstructured Seasonal Data

Flowering times sat as free text in the published stock list, making it impossible to plan propagation activity by season at a glance.

Disconnected Council Demand

Local council revegetation planting lists existed separately from the nursery's own stock, with no easy way to flag which species mattered most.

Methodology

How It Was Built

01

Load & Clean the Dataset

Used Power Query to strip non-data rows from the raw stock list, promote real headers, extract category labels via a conditional column and fill-down, then standardise flowering time and price code into clean split columns.

02

Season & Council Reference Tables

Built a season lookup table and linked it to each species' flowering start via XLOOKUP. Manually cross-referenced a sample of council revegetation lists to flag council-recommended species.

03

Power BI Data Model

Imported the cleaned Excel tables into Power BI and verified relationships between species, season and council demand data so they could be used together across the dashboard.

04

What-If Parameters & DAX

Created adjustable parameters for strike rate, potting-on lead time and bench space, then wrote DAX measures to forecast available units, required bench space and the resulting capacity gap.

05

KPI Cards & Visuals

Built KPI cards, a seasonal stock chart, a category breakdown chart and a conditionally-formatted capacity gap card that updates live as the what-if sliders move.

06

Documentation & Handoff

Wrote an assumptions register documenting the value, source, date and confidence level behind every planning input, plus a one-page refresh guide so a volunteer coordinator could update it unassisted.
